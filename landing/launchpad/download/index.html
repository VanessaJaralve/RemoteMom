A LITTLE HELP GETTING STARTED

Need help with your PDF?

Email Vanessa at vanessa.jaralve@gmail.com. You can copy the address into your email app if the link does not open.

Email help is handled manually; there is no guaranteed response time during this early version. If you are still blocked, reply in the same email thread so the details stay together.

I cannot find or open the PDF

Check your device’s Downloads or Files folder. If the file is not there, try Download the PDF again, or open the PDF in a new tab and use your browser’s save or download option.

If you are browsing inside a social app, try this page in your usual browser. Open the saved file in a PDF reader. If it still will not open, email Vanessa with the file version above and the error you saw.

Will the kit arrive by email?

The kit is provided as a direct download on this page. An automated kit email is not sent. You do not need to sign up again just to retry this download.

What should I include when I ask for help?

Include this page address, the file version, approximate time, your device and browser, the message you saw, and what you tried. Hide completed form fields and personal details in screenshots.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government IDs, or private child, health, family, or employer information. Support messages are not permission to send marketing or publish a testimonial.
