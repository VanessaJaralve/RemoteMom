A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear expectations,
from the start.

Is this a job board or a recruitment service?

No. Launchpad provides educational resources to help you explore roles, assess working conditions, and take focused steps. It doesn’t list or place people in jobs.

What if I’m already working remotely?

Start with Manage Remote Life. Focus on work blocks, boundaries, interruptions, and how your current arrangement fits your family.

Does this guarantee work or a safe opportunity?

No resource can guarantee employment, income, flexibility, or that an opportunity is legitimate. Verify independently through the employer’s official channels and relevant authorities in your country before sharing information or making commitments.

Is Launchpad the RemoteMom planning app?

They’re separate offers. Launchpad is an educational career and work-life resource. RemoteMom is the daily-planning app for managing work and family responsibilities.
